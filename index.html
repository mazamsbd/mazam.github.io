About Me

Researcher & Materials Scientist

Professional Profile

I am a researcher working at the intersection of polymer science, electrochemistry, membrane technology, and hydrogen energy. My work aims to address key challenges in the hydrogen energy sector by developing high-performance, durable, and cost-effective membrane materials.


My expertise lies in the design, synthesis, and functionalization of novel polymeric materials. I specialize in tailoring the morphology and microstructure of polymer membranes to optimize their ionic conductivity, chemical stability, and mechanical strength, which are essential for achieving efficient and durable electrochemical performance.
